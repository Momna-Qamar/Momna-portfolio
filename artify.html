Artify AI

Image generation · Web app

An AI-assisted creative workspace that turns ideas into stunning visuals.

Overview

Artify AI is a web application that lets users generate high-quality images from text prompts. I designed it for creators, marketers and designers who need visuals fast, without needing advanced design skills.

The problem

Most AI image tools feel technical and overwhelming. Beginners don't know how to write good prompts, and there's no simple way to organize or refine results.
