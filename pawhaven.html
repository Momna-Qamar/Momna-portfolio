PawHaven

Find a friend who needs you.

A pet adoption app that matches people with rescued cats and dogs through a short quiz, so every pet finds a home that truly fits.

Role
UI/UX Designer
Platform
iOS & Android
Tools

About the project

01

Overview

PawHaven connects people looking to adopt with rescued pets. A short quiz learns what kind of pet suits the user's life and shows matches they can save, meet and adopt.

02

The problem

Adoption listings are scattered across social media and shelter sites. People scroll endlessly, don't know which pet suits their home, and give up before they find the right one.

03

The solution

A warm, friendly app that asks a few simple questions, then shows pets that match. Clear profiles, favorites and a one-tap adoption start make the journey easy.

Design process

  1. 1

    Research

    Talked to adopters and studied shelter websites to find what slows people down.

  2. 2

    User flow

    Mapped the journey from the matching quiz to starting an adoption.

  3. 3

    Wireframes

    Sketched the quiz, match list, pet profile and favorites layouts.

  4. 4

    Visual design

    Chose a warm coral and cream palette with Poppins and Inter to feel caring and friendly.

  5. 5

    Prototype

    Built an interactive prototype and tested the quiz flow with real users.

Key features

Match

A quick quiz finds your perfect match

Users answer 4 simple questions about the pet they want and their lifestyle. A progress bar shows how far along they are, so the quiz never feels long.

Discover

Matches that fit your life

Results show each pet's age, gender, breed and location, plus a short note on their personality, so users can compare at a glance.

Profile

Everything you need to know

Each profile shows the pet's story, temperament and health details like vaccination and deworming, with a clear button to start the adoption.

Save

Keep your favorites close

Tap the heart to save a pet. Favorites keep every pet a user loves in one place, ready to meet when they're ready.
